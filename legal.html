Terms of Use

Rewixa is a rewards app. You collect points by claiming a daily reward, spinning a wheel, opening a scratch card, completing challenges and partner offers, and inviting friends.

Points have no cash value. They cannot be bought, sold or transferred, and can only be redeemed for the rewards listed in the app. Rewards are reviewed and delivered manually, depend on availability and may change. Rewixa is not a gambling service and no real money is wagered.

One account per person. Cheating, exploiting bugs, using multiple accounts or automation may lead to loss of points or removal of the account. Rewixa is not affiliated with any brand mentioned as an example reward.

Privacy Policy

Data we collect: email address, name, and your activity in the app (points, spins, claims, reward requests, invite relationships), plus the contact you enter to receive a reward.

Why: to run your account, credit and track points, deliver rewards, prevent fraud and provide support.

Who processes it: our backend provider Supabase. If we add advertising or offer partners, they are listed here before use. We do not sell your personal data.

Retention: until you delete your account. Your rights: access, correction and deletion, by email or in the app.

Delete your account

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account, points, history and reward requests.

Cannot open the app? Email us from your registered address and we will delete your account and data within 30 days.
